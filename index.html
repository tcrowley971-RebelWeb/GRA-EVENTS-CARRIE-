Questions, answered

Where are you based and do you post nationwide?

We are based in Cork and cover Cork and Munster. Signage can be posted anywhere in Ireland.

What materials do you make signs in?

Linen, lace, cotton, silk and acrylic. Every sign is personalised with your names, date and wording.

What occasions do you make signage for?

Weddings, christenings, engagements and celebrations of all kinds. If you have something in mind, just ask.

How do I enquire or order?

Send us a direct message on Instagram at @graevents with your date, venue and what you have in mind.
